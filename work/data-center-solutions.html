Data Center Solutions

An LG.com B2B project that conveys complex technical information through visual content linking key data center spaces with cooling solutions, supported by product information modules.

Client / Platform
LG Electronics / LG.com B2B
Period
2026
Role
B2B Digital Content Design · Visual asset development · UI content production
Contribution
Created and refined the data center aerial visual and product graphics, and contributed to key content modules including Key Features, Product Lineup and product detail popovers.
Deliverables
Data center aerial visual · Key Features pictograms · Product Lineup content · Four product detail popovers · Product and cooling solution imagery

Context

Project requirements

  • Global guidelines — LG.com B2B content standards
  • Schedule — revisions driven by feedback and changes in production direction
  • Multiple stakeholders — client review and collaboration
  • Accuracy in representing technical products and equipment

My Role

What I did

After an initial collaborative phase, I worked on individual deliverables. My contribution included:

  • Creating visuals and assets
  • Designing the Key Features pictograms
  • Designing Product Lineup content
  • Working on the product detail popovers
  • Generating, compositing and retouching AI imagery
  • Revising designs based on feedback

Judgment & proposals

  • I kept the aerial angle confirmed by the client while improving perspective, equipment placement and image detail.
  • I revised the visuals so that the relationship between key data center facilities and cooling equipment reads naturally.
  • I restructured and revised the Product Lineup and popover content in response to changes in production direction.

Guidelines applied

LG.com B2B brand and web content standards.

Design Approach

  1. Reviewing requirements and reference materials
  2. Refining the visual direction
  3. Producing content modules and graphics
  4. Reflecting feedback

What I Designed

Final Deliverables

Outcome

  • Key Features, Product Lineup and product detail popover deliverables produced
  • Four product detail popovers

Project Notes

Conveying complex data center cooling solutions required showing the relationship between products and space accurately. I kept the composition the client had approved while improving equipment placement and perspective, and produced the product information modules and detail popovers.

Through changes in direction and repeated feedback, I kept both the accuracy and the visual consistency of the B2B technical content in mind.
