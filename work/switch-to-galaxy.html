Switch to Galaxy

A Samsung.com marketing page that reduces the psychological and practical friction of switching from iPhone to Galaxy, presenting key information such as data transfer and cross-platform compatibility as intuitive digital content.

Client / Platform
Samsung Electronics / Samsung.com
Period
Jun – Oct 2024
Role
Project Leader · KV concept proposal · Digital content design
Contribution
Led the team through an unclear production direction and a shifting schedule, proposed a KV concept ahead of confirmation, and delivered three page launches and content refinements once the strategy was set.
Deliverables
Samsung.com marketing page · Digital content and key feature visuals · Desktop page designs · Phased launch content

Context

Project requirements

  • Global guidelines — Samsung.com brand expression standards
  • Schedule — a fluid production direction and phased launches
  • Multiple stakeholders — client requests and team collaboration

My Role

What I did

My contribution included:

  • Proposing the visual / KV concept
  • Designing digital content and producing assets
  • Leading the project
  • Reflecting client feedback in each launch

Judgment & proposals

  • I proposed a concept and KV when the direction was unclear and the schedule was shrinking after kickoff. The proposal was not adopted in the end.
  • I expressed message and emoji compatibility between iOS and Android with minimal, easy-to-read visual elements.
  • I adjusted content in phases to match the confirmed strategy and client requests, and handled three launches.

Guidelines applied

Samsung.com global brand and content design standards.

Design Approach

  1. Reviewing requirements and guidelines
  2. Proposing a concept to set the direction
  3. Producing content and visuals
  4. Reflecting feedback

What I Designed

Final Deliverables

Outcome

  • Three launches completed
  • Content revised and improved based on client feedback

Project Notes

The production direction was unclear at the start and the schedule was shortened, so I proposed a concept and KV ahead of confirmation. The proposal was not adopted in the end.

Based on the direction that was confirmed, I organized the key information of the Galaxy switching journey into visuals that are easy to understand. Through three launches I reflected client feedback and responded to changing requirements.
